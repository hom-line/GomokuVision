# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in GomokuVision. The repository is currently a release candidate awaiting a confirmed open-source license. Until that license is added, do not assume redistribution or derivative-use permissions.

For a proposed change, explain the target engine and ROI/display mode, provide a reproducible synthetic input, include a regression test, and state whether the change affects coordinate conventions or the synchronization timeline. Keep original third-party footage, personal files, and licensed MATLAB binaries out of commits.
